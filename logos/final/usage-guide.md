# Cuppet Courier logo usage

## Rationale

Courier presents every Cuppet agent as a contact that carries delegated work away and returns with a message. Its origami construction makes the mark feel active and crafted without relying on a chatbot bubble, letter monogram, or generic AI sparkle.

## Preferred files

- Use `horizontal/cuppet-horizontal-full-color.svg` for website headers, documents, and wide placements on light backgrounds.
- Use `icon/cuppet-app-icon.svg` for the mobile app icon, avatars, and square placements.
- Use `stacked/cuppet-stacked-full-color.svg` for centered or narrow layouts.
- Use `horizontal/cuppet-horizontal-on-dark.svg` on dark backgrounds.
- Use the monochrome icon variants only when full color is unavailable or inappropriate.

## Palette

- Warm ivory: `#F5F3EE`
- Ink: `#17201C`
- Coral: `#FF9A7F` to `#C94F66`
- Indigo: `#5D579D` to `#29264F`
- Teal: `#0B625B` to `#2BA898`
- Amber: `#F4C66D` to `#DB8833`

The gradients are part of the full-color identity. Do not replace individual facets with unrelated colors.

## Typography

The exploratory wordmark uses Avenir Next at weight 700, with Avenir and Arial as fallbacks. Avenir Next availability and licensing vary by platform. Before a public brand release, outline the approved wordmark or bundle a properly licensed typeface.

## Size and clear space

- The full-color icon remains recognizable at 24 px high; prefer at least 32 px when the individual facets should remain visible.
- Keep clear space around the mark equal to at least four eye diameters.
- Do not place text, controls, borders, or other logos inside that clear-space area.

## Backgrounds

- Use the primary full-color logo on warm ivory, white, or similarly light neutral backgrounds.
- Use the dedicated on-dark horizontal logo or light monochrome icon on dark backgrounds.
- Use the app-icon file as supplied; its warm ivory tile is intentional.

## Avoid

- Do not stretch, rotate, skew, or rearrange the bird’s facets.
- Do not add shadows, outlines, glows, or extra gradients.
- Do not recolor individual facets outside the approved palette.
- Do not place the primary logo over photography or patterns that hide the silhouette.
- Do not crop the wings, tail, or beak.

The mark is original artwork but has not undergone a formal trademark clearance search.
